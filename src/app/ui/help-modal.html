How to use this app

Shadowing is the simplest way to learn English chunks: pick an activity, listen to each sentence, and repeat it aloud in the pause after it.

  1. Choose an activity on the home screen, then a topic and how long you have, and press Start.
  2. Press Play and repeat every sentence aloud during the gap.
  3. Tune speed, gap and voice to your level.

What each feature is for

  • Auto Play / Pause (space) — start and stop the exercise. When paused, ← repeats the current sentence from the start.
  • Next (→) — jump ahead whenever you want.
  • Session (5/10/15 min) — chosen on the home screen before you start; the app tracks your time and counts the sentences you practiced.
  • options — hides the settings you tune once and then leave alone: shuffle, blur, retry until 5★, and the speed, gap and voice controls.
  • Blur — hide the sentences while you practice, to build listening memory; already-practiced lines reappear so you can check yourself.
  • speed — slow it down to catch every sound, or speed it up when you feel ready.
  • gap — the pause between sentences is your time to repeat; make it longer when you need more time.
  • voice — pick the English voice that is easiest to understand (Microsoft Edge has the best selection).
  • My text — the activity where you paste or type your own text, and the app splits it into sentences you can practice like any other topic. It is kept on this device only, and is read back exactly as you wrote it.
  • Shuffle — randomize the order so you practice without memorizing the sequence.
  • Speaking — the activity that transcribes what you say and scores it 0–5 stars. It listens until you stop talking, and each line keeps its score. Click any sentence to have it read out and scored on demand. It needs the microphone: if you refuse the prompt the app says so and the session runs unscored.
  • Spelling — the activity that reads a sentence and asks you to write the sentence back from memory, then scores your spelling and points out the misspelled or missing words.
  • Check — only My text lets you choose what is scored, because it is your own content: pick Nothing, Speaking or Spelling. Every other activity already is that choice.
  • retry until 5★ — when a sentence is being scored, one under 5 stars is read again for another try, up to five times. Press → or click another sentence to move on sooner.
